Reminders that chase you

Meetings, deadlines, callbacks, rent, drugs. Say it like you would say it to a person — once, or every day, every Monday, every weekday, every month on the 5th.

Questions

Straight answers

Will it answer any question I ask?

No — and that is on purpose. Doni is not a general chat bot. It handles reminders, expense records, documents, recordings, shop media and rates. Ask it who won a match in 1998 and it will tell you plainly that this is outside what it does. That is the deal: a tool that does a short list of jobs properly, not a talking machine that does everything badly.

Do I need to download anything or open an account?

No. Save the number +234 915 326 4893, or scan the QR code at the top, and send a message. There is no app, no sign-up form, no password and no card required.

What does it cost?

Free, with a daily allowance that covers normal use — reminders, records, documents and rates are unlimited, plus 10 pictures and 3 short videos a day. Longer, sharper videos will be pay-per-use from ₦1,999 each: you buy one and own it, no subscription. Payments are not open yet, so today nothing can be charged.

Can Doni see my other chats?

No. Doni only ever sees the messages you send to its own number, exactly like any other contact in your phone. It cannot read your other conversations, your groups or your contact list, and it never sends messages to anyone on your behalf.

What language should I write in?

English is best, and Nigerian English works fine — "omo I just burn 8500 on fuel o" is understood as an expense, not a riddle. Doni replies in the language you wrote in.
