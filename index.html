Questions

Straight answers

Will it answer any question I ask?

Some. Doni answers up to 20 quick questions a day in WhatsApp, and more in the computer version. It is not a general chat bot: it handles reminders, records, documents, recordings and shop media, and it will say so plainly when something is outside what it does. A tool that does a short list of jobs properly, not a talking machine that does everything badly.

Do I need to download anything or open an account?

No. Save the number +234 915 326 4893, or scan the QR code at the top, and send a message. There is no app, no sign-up form, no password and no card required.

What does it cost?

Nothing today. Every account starts with 2,000 tokens for pictures and videos. Reminders, records, documents, voice notes and translation never use tokens. Paid top-ups are not open yet, so today nothing can be charged.

Can Doni see my other chats?

No. Doni only ever sees the messages you send to its own number, exactly like any other contact in your phone. It cannot read your other conversations, your groups or your contact list, and it never sends messages to anyone on your behalf.

Can I change a poster after I get it?

Yes. Reply to it with what to change: "make the price bigger", "change Saturday to Sunday", "use my logo", and Doni redoes it. Send your own photo and it works from that instead of inventing a product.

What language should I write in?

English is best, and Nigerian English works fine, "omo I just burn 8500 on fuel o" is understood as an expense, not a riddle. Doni replies in the language you wrote in.
